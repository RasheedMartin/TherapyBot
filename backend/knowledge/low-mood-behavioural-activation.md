# Low mood and behavioural activation

## The low-mood cycle
When mood drops, people often withdraw and do less. Doing less removes sources of enjoyment and accomplishment, which lowers mood further. Behavioural activation breaks this cycle by acting first, without waiting until motivation returns.

## Getting started
- List activities that used to bring pleasure, connection, or a sense of achievement, plus routine tasks that have piled up.
- Pick one small, specific activity ("walk to the corner and back after lunch," not "exercise more").
- Schedule it at a set time, then notice your mood before and after (0 to 10).
- Build gradually. Small wins count.

## When motivation is very low
Shrink the step until it feels almost too easy: put on shoes, open the document, text one friend. Expect it to feel effortful at first; the mood lift usually follows action rather than preceding it.

## When to seek more help
If low mood lasts most days for two weeks or more, affects sleep, appetite, concentration, or the ability to function, or comes with thoughts of death or self-harm, it is important to talk to a doctor or mental health professional. If there are thoughts of suicide, call or text 988 in the US or contact local emergency services.
