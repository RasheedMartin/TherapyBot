# Getting through intense emotions (DBT skills)

## What DBT offers
Dialectical behaviour therapy combines acceptance and change. It teaches skills in four areas: mindfulness, distress tolerance, emotion regulation, and interpersonal effectiveness. Distress tolerance skills help people get through a crisis moment without making things worse.

## STOP
**S**top: freeze for a moment. **T**ake a step back: breathe and don't act immediately. **O**bserve: notice what is happening inside and around you. **P**roceed mindfully: choose an action that fits your goals. Useful when about to react impulsively.

## TIPP for very high emotion
- **Temperature**: cool water on the face can quickly lower arousal.
- **Intense exercise**: a short burst of activity burns off physical tension.
- **Paced breathing**: slow the exhale.
- **Paired muscle relaxation**: tense and release muscles while breathing out.
People with heart conditions or eating disorders should check with a doctor before using the temperature or intense-exercise skills.

## Self-soothing and distraction
Using the five senses to comfort yourself (a warm drink, music, a soft blanket) or temporarily shifting attention (a puzzle, a show, helping someone) can help ride out an emotional wave. Emotions peak and then pass.

## Radical acceptance
Accepting reality as it is, without approving of it, can reduce the extra suffering that comes from fighting what cannot be changed. Acceptance is often the first step toward changing what can be changed.
